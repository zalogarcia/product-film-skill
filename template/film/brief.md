# Brief: Fernwise example film

Fernwise is a FICTIONAL product, invented for this example. Replace this file with your own brief.

## The product in one sentence

A phone app that tells you which houseplant to water, when, and how much.

## What it does (the only facts the film may use)

1. It keeps a watering schedule for each plant, worked out from the species, the pot size and when the plant was last watered.
2. It sends one notification per watering, with the amount in millilitres.
3. Its "This week" screen lists the week's waterings, one row per plant, with the day and the amount.

## Who it is for

People with a few houseplants who keep forgetting them, or drowning them. Not botanists.

## The one thing the viewer should leave with

"It tells me exactly when and how much, so I stop guessing."

## The call to action

The product's name and address on the end card. No price.

## Cuts

- Master: 16:9, 1920x1080, 18 s, captions burned in.
- Vertical: 9:16, 1080x1920, 18 s, captions burned in for sound off viewing, loops seamlessly.
- Teaser: about 10 s, cut from the vertical.
