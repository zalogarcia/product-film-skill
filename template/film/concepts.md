# Concepts and the critic pass

Three concepts, then a critic pass that tries to kill each hook. The one that survives is the film.

## A. "Thirsty since Tuesday" (picked)

Open on a drooping fern and one fact: last watered Tuesday. The app nudges with the exact amount, the fern recovers, the week's list shows every plant handled.

## B. "The plant graveyard"

A shelf of dead plants, one after another, then the app.

## C. "Feature tour"

The app's screens one by one: schedule, reminders, the weekly list, settings.

## Critic pass

- **C dies first.** A tour of screens is a feature list, not a story, and nobody stops scrolling for a settings screen.
- **B dies on the hook.** A graveyard is a mood, not a problem the viewer recognizes in their own home in one second; it also opens on loss, and the product cannot bring the plants back.
- **A survives, with changes.** The critic's attacks: (1) "Tuesday" must be on screen as type in the first 2 s, or the hook is only a sad plant; (2) the nudge must show the amount, because "water your fern" is what every reminder app says and the amount is the product's edge; (3) the list must not show a number the brief cannot back, so no "98% of plants saved" style stat.

## Why A

The problem is visible in frame 0 without a word, the turn is one notification, and every beat after it is a consequence of the product doing its one job.
